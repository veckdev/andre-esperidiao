Work About Contact

Based in Dublin, working across Europe and the UK. Fashion and lifestyle photographer since 2010.

A visual artist observing human presence and the behaviour of light. My work is shaped by minimalism, form, geometry and a classical sensibility, informed by past eras and the origins of photography. Drawing from photographic heritage and timeless image-making, I move between commercial and fashion narratives.

Collaborations include designers, fashion creatives, brands, and contributors to Vogue, L’Officiel and various international publications.
